ABOUT ALS

Opening Doors to New Opportunities

The Alternative Learning System (ALS) provides an alternative pathway to basic education for learners who cannot participate in or complete the formal school system.

Through flexible and community-based learning, ALS helps individuals develop essential knowledge, skills, and competencies that can support further education, employment, entrepreneurship, and lifelong learning.
